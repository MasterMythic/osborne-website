About

About Osborne.

Osborne helps businesses protect their operations, digital identity and customer trust. Our cybersecurity work assesses risks, strengthens agreed controls and supports detection, response and recovery within the contracted scope. Our brand protection work finds suspected online abuse, investigates evidence and manages appropriate provider reports. Both services give customers clear actions, accountable follow-up and verified results.

Experience

Our team has over 10 years of experience delivering software services for organisations in the US defence sector, logistics, blockchain and gaming.

Why both services

One relationship, two workstreams.

You get one customer relationship and shared case references, with distinct cybersecurity and brand protection workstreams.

The cybersecurity workstream handles your company systems and access. The brand protection workstream handles outside misuse of your identity and the reports made to providers. When a campaign affects both, we link the evidence so you can understand it as one problem.
