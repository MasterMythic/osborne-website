Brand protection

Protect your brand and customers from online impersonation.

We help you find and respond to fake websites, impersonating accounts, scam promotions and suspected counterfeit listings. We investigate the evidence, manage reports to the relevant providers and check what action has been taken.

Request a brand protection review → See the process

01 · The problem

Scammers don't need your passwords.

Scammers can misuse your name, logo, products or identity without ever touching your own systems. Brand protection focuses on that outside activity. We establish your legitimate online presence, monitor the agreed channels and investigate findings that could mislead your customers or infringe your rights.

When a case is supported by evidence, we prepare the relevant reports and pursue the organisations able to act: a hosting provider, platform, domain provider, marketplace, advertising service or payment provider, depending on the case. Each provider makes its own decision under its own policies.

06 · Questions

Brand protection questions.

What is online brand protection?

A process for finding, investigating and responding to online activity that impersonates your brand, misleads your customers or infringes your rights. It includes gathering evidence, reporting to the right providers and checking the result.

What can you help us report?

Depending on the agreed scope and the evidence, cases can include fake websites, phishing pages, impersonating social accounts, scam ads, counterfeit listings and misuse of protected content.

Will a fake website definitely be taken down?

No one can promise that. The provider or platform makes its own enforcement decision. We manage the case and report the confirmed outcome and any remaining exposure.

Why contact more than one provider?

A scam may depend on a domain, a website, an ad, a social account and a payment service. Different organisations control each of those, so several reporting routes can apply to the same case.

Do payment restrictions mean the website is gone?

No. A payment provider can decide whether to keep serving a merchant, but it can't take the website down. We report payment action separately from content removal.

Will you report every seller using our brand name?

No. We look at the actual conduct and the relevant rights or policy. Genuine resale or a lawful reference to your products isn't counterfeiting just because the seller is unfamiliar.

What do you need from us?

Your official brand references, a named contact, relevant rights information and written authority to act. A list of your legitimate accounts and partners helps us tell real activity from impersonation.

Who decides what gets reported?

Each case is reviewed before reports are submitted. Uncertain findings, disputed rights and legal declarations get further review, and nothing is reported in your name without your authority.

How quickly will action happen?

It depends on the evidence, the type of case and the provider's own process. We prioritise active harm to your customers and keep you updated on the stage reached. Any response commitments are set out in your agreement.

What happens if the abuse comes back?

Within the agreed scope, we watch for restored or replacement pages, domains and accounts. We assess new findings, keep the case history and pursue fresh action when it's justified.
