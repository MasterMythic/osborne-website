Cybersecurity & brand protection

In an AI world, anyone can be a hacker.

Osborne helps businesses protect what attackers now find easiest to copy, fake and exploit: the systems you run on, the brand your customers recognise, the IP you've built and the trust that holds it all together.

01 · The shift

The skill barrier is gone. The damage isn't.

Attacks that once took a practised criminal days now take anyone with an AI tool a few minutes. The methods haven't changed much. The number of people able to use them has.

  1. A

    A convincing phishing email

    Needed someone who wrote well in your customers' language and knew how your company sounds.

    Generated in seconds, in any language, in your tone of voice, and personalised for each recipient.

  2. B

    A copy of your website

    Took a designer and a developer time to rebuild your pages well enough to fool a customer.

    A lookalike site, payment page or login screen can be produced from a few screenshots.

  3. C

    A fake account in your name

    Took effort to keep up: posting, replying and looking real over weeks.

    Profiles, posts and replies can be generated and run at scale, across many platforms at once.

  4. D

    A voice or face you trust

    Impersonating an executive meant finding an actor and hoping no one looked too closely.

    Short samples of a real voice or video can be used to create a convincing imitation.

  5. E

    Finding a way in

    Searching for weak configurations and forgotten systems was slow, technical work.

    Tools help less experienced attackers search for and probe weak points faster.

02 · What we protect

Four layers. One business.

A breach rarely stays in one place. A stolen login becomes a fake invoice, a fake invoice becomes a damaged reputation, and a damaged reputation costs you customers. We look at the layers together.

  • i

    Systems

    The accounts, email, devices, cloud services and data your business runs on. We review access and sign-in protection, email authentication, patching, backups you can actually restore, and whether anyone would notice an intrusion.

  • ii

    Intellectual property

    Developing

    Designs, content, product information and know-how. We help you map what you own, where it appears and where it's exposed, so you and your counsel can decide what to act on.

  • iii

    Brand

    Developing

    Your name, logo and likeness online. We look for lookalike domains, impersonating accounts and scam pages that borrow your identity, and keep the evidence organised.

  • iv

    Customer trust

    The confidence that a message, page or call from you is really from you. We help you make it easier for customers to recognise the real thing and report a fake one.

Brand and IP protection is a service we are still building. Each engagement is scoped individually, and we will tell you plainly what is and isn't covered. We do not provide legal advice; enforcement decisions sit with you and your counsel.

03 · Approach

Calm, scoped, and on the record.

Security work should lower your stress, not add to it. Every engagement follows the same five steps, and you see each one.

  1. 1

    Scope

    We agree in writing what we look at, what we don't, who to contact and how sensitive information is handled.

  2. 2

    Assess

    We review your systems and your public footprint with your authority. Nothing is tested without written permission.

  3. 3

    Prioritise

    We rank what we find by likely harm to your business, not by how technical it sounds.

  4. 4

    Act with you

    We help you fix, block or escalate, with each action approved by you before it happens.

  5. 5

    Report plainly

    You get a clear account of what we found, what was done and what is still open, in language your board can read.

What you can hold us to

  • Authorised work only. We never test a system without its owner's written permission, and we never "hack back".
  • Facts kept apart from guesses. Our reports say what we confirmed, what we suspect and how sure we are.
  • No promises we can't evidence. A complaint filed is not a fake page removed, and a page removed is not permanent safety. We report each one for what it is.

04 · Engagements

Three ways to start.

For most businesses

Readiness review

A structured look at the basics attackers rely on you missing: access, email, updates, backups and visibility. You leave with a prioritised list, not a pile of jargon.

  • Scoping call and written scope
  • Review against agreed areas
  • Prioritised findings and next steps

For brands customers search for

Brand exposure check Developing

A first look at how your name is being used online: lookalike domains, impersonating profiles and scam pages, with evidence organised for you and your counsel.

  • Agreed brands, names and channels
  • Evidence recorded as found
  • Options for each case, approved by you

When something feels wrong

A specific concern

A suspicious message, a strange login, a fake page in your name. Tell us what you've seen and we'll scope a proportionate response.

  • Describe what you noticed
  • Agree what we look at
  • Clear next actions

Under active attack right now? Don't wait on a brief. Disconnect affected systems where it's safe to do so, contact your IT or incident response provider, and report crime to the relevant authorities.
