02 · The shift

The skill barrier is gone. The damage isn't.

Attacks that once took a practised criminal days now take anyone with an AI tool a few minutes. The methods haven't changed much. The number of people able to use them has.

  1. A

    A convincing phishing email

    Needed someone who wrote well in your customers' language and knew how your company sounds.

    Generated in seconds, in any language, in your tone of voice, and personalised for each recipient.

  2. B

    A copy of your website

    Took a designer and a developer time to rebuild your pages well enough to fool a customer.

    A lookalike site, payment page or login screen can be produced from a few screenshots.

  3. C

    A fake account in your name

    Took effort to keep up: posting, replying and looking real over weeks.

    Profiles, posts and replies can be generated and run at scale, across many platforms at once.

  4. D

    A voice or face you trust

    Impersonating an executive meant finding an actor and hoping no one looked too closely.

    Short samples of a real voice or video can be used to create a convincing imitation.

  5. E

    Finding a way in

    Searching for weak configurations and forgotten systems was slow, technical work.

    Tools help less experienced attackers search for and probe weak points faster.

03 · Working together

One case. Both sides covered.

A scam rarely stays in one place. A fake login page in your name is a brand problem; the staff accounts it targets are a security problem. You get one case reference, and when a campaign touches both, we link the evidence.

  • i

    Systems

    Cybersecurity

    The accounts, email, devices, cloud services and data your business runs on. We assess them, agree improvements and check the changes work.

  • ii

    Intellectual property

    Brand protection

    Your protected works, product images and trademarks. We record them as reference points and use the appropriate rights-based reporting route when misuse is established.

  • iii

    Brand

    Brand protection

    Your name, logo and official channels. We investigate lookalike domains, impersonating accounts, scam ads and fake stores, and pursue the providers able to act.

  • iv

    Customer trust

    Both

    The confidence that a message or page from you is really from you. Secure email and accounts on the inside; reported impersonation on the outside.

Each engagement is scoped individually, and we tell you plainly what is and isn't covered. We don't provide legal advice; rights disputes and legal decisions stay with you and your advisers.
